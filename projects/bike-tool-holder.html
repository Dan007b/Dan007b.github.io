Bike Tool Holder

Overview

I designed and manufactured a multitool holder for my bike.

Design & Challenges

I was tired of putting the tool in my pocket, so I made a holder to put it on the bike. I designed it in Fusion360, and manufactured with a CNC plasma cutter.

Results

Final design was made from a sheet of aluminum. It works great, and I am still using it today.
